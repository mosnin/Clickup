# Truthful choice

Use specificity and a bounded demonstration to reduce uncertainty. A guide gives the reader a useful procedure before asking for a purchase. Plan comparison exposes scope and units rather than a fabricated reference price. Company or runtime logos are not proof of customer adoption. Sample workflows are labeled examples and do not speak as invented customers.

No countdowns, invented scarcity, fabricated endorsements, automatic purchase or hidden overage. A demo request is a request, not a booking. Measure comprehension of the product job, the cost unit and the next step before optimizing click-through.

Self-review applies the requested skills; no independent agent, Company OS or Symbolic verification is claimed.
